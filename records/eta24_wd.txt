2. Method
---------
Total. `eta24 campaign total --heuristic wd --md-max 40 --level-draws 10000000
--uniform-draws 2000000000` (9 min wall, seed 1). Placements are drawn
uniformly within each Manhattan level m <= 40 and weighted by the level's exact
count (eta::md_levels); states above MD 40 come from uniform solvable draws.
No distance is searched: the estimator needs only WD on sampled states.
  levels MD <= 40      1.7534e-20 ± 0.03%
  uniform MD > 40      1.0470e-21 ± 1.6%
The same command for MD reproduces the exact MD total to -0.013% (-0.7 SE;
records/eta24_md.txt section 6c).

Tail d >= 65. The level-stratified tail draws of records/eta24_md.txt section
6d, scored for WD (`campaign score --heuristic wd`): levels m <= 40 with states
proven >= 65 by the zero-aware PDB search, plus the uniform tail run's draws
above MD 40.
  weighting   levels MD <= 40       uniform draws MD > 40    tail
  uniform     1.5396e-21 ± 4.9%     8.5801e-22 ± 2.9%        2.3976e-21 ± 3.3%
  tree        1.5452e-21 ± 5.1%     8.6783e-22 ± 2.7%        2.4130e-21 ± 3.4%
  degree      1.5482e-21 ± 5.1%     8.6919e-22 ± 2.8%        2.4174e-21 ± 3.4%
The uniform tail run alone gave 2.3389e-21 ± 27.5%. Within the levels, 35..40
carry 11-15% of the level part each and m <= 30 carry 3.8% together.

Per sphere. k <= 24: exact breadth-first layers (`campaign layers --heuristic
wd --max-k 24`, 20 s). k = 25..64: the MD campaign's 3,311,788 stored boards
and reach probabilities rescored with WD (`campaign score --heuristic wd`).

5. Reading
----------
1. eta_WD = 1.858e-20 ± 0.09% (uniform), 5.39 times smaller than eta_MD. In
   the thesis's reading of eta ratios, IDA* with walking distance expands
   about 5.4 times fewer nodes than with Manhattan distance on hard instances
   (5.1 under the tree and degree weightings).
2. WD and MD give equal eta_k for k <= 5. WD's eta_k is 0.25-0.37 of MD's on
   the exact spheres k = 12..24 and 0.14-0.33 on the sampled spheres 25..64.
3. Per-sphere eta_WD lies between 5.8e-22 and 9.8e-22 for k = 53..64, with no
   rise beyond the intervals. 12.9% of eta_WD lies at d >= 65.
4. For WD the sphere samples are 3% low over 0..64 (-0.7 SE, within their
   interval); for MD the same samples are 11% low (-2.9 SE). The level totals
   do not depend on the sphere samples, so the totals in section 1 carry
   neither shortfall.
