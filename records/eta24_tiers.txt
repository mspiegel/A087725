======================================================================================

Heuristics, as the solver prunes with them (src/puzzle24/search/engine.rs):
  cWD    escape-constrained walking distance (data/cwd_mm.bin)

records/eta24_md.txt. Campaign data in data/eta24_md (git-ignored).

2. Evaluating the tiers on single boards
----------------------------------------
engine::TierEval lays a board down as the engine's depth-0 frame, lets depth
1 share its axis slots and tracked tile lines, and calls the engine's own
lm2_child and lm1l_child under the same conditions as run_iteration; the
search loop is untouched. The goal board is 0 under every tier (the search
never consults a tier there).

Checks:
  - engine::tests::tier_eval_matches_cwd_is_ordered_and_admissible
    (feature cwd-table-tests; passes in release and in debug, where the
    engine's tracked-line drift assertion runs): cWD from the mmap artifact
    equals Cwd::eval on the merged map; cWD <= LM2 <= cLM2; cLM2 <= true
    distance on all 18,174 boards within 11 moves and the 63 solved oracle
    cases; on 20,000 200-step walks LM2 > cWD on 1,537 boards and cLM2 > LM2
    on 3,713.
  - Node-count canary unchanged: `solve24 --config large --position R
    --prove-at-least 145 --clm2 --zpdb8 --parallel` -> 115,436,814 nodes.
  - Consistency (scratch, 2,000,000 random-walk boards, 6,700,761 neighbour
    pairs):
      |h(s) - h(s')| > 1:  cWD 0, LM2 3,967 (largest 3), cLM2 0
      h parity different from the blank-cell parity: 0 for all three
    LM2 is not consistent: 0.06% of neighbour pairs differ by 3. eta's
    reading as an IDA* node-count factor assumes a consistent heuristic, so
    for LM2 it is an approximation; cWD and cLM2 showed no violation.

3. Method and cost
------------------
Per heuristic h, in sequence (`data/eta24_md/tiers_run.log`):
  campaign layers --max-k 24 --heuristic h     exact eta_k, k <= 24 (40-60 s)
  campaign score --heuristic h                 spheres 25..64 rescored from the
                                               MD campaign's 3,311,788 boards;
                                               tail rescored (below)
  campaign total --heuristic h --md-max 48 --level-draws 4000000
                 --uniform-draws 1000000000    total by Manhattan levels (5-8 min)
  campaign total --heuristic h --md-max 40 --level-draws 16384
                 --distance-min 25 --distance-max 40    band 25..40 (4 min)
Wall clock per heuristic 11 min (cWD, LM2) and 14 min (cLM2).

Total. `campaign total` draws every level first and frees the 12.2 GiB level
table before the heuristic's tables load (cLM2's are 16.5 GB). Levels up to
MD 48: above MD 40, uniform draws were heavy-tailed for cWD (±49% with 1e7
draws); above MD 48 the uniform part is 0.4% of the total (±1.9% with 1e8).

Tail d >= 65. Rescored from stored boards: the level-stratified hits of
records/eta24_md.txt section 6 (every hit is stored) for MD <= 40, and the
uniform tail run's stored boards (all accepted boards with MD <= 72) above.
Unstored accepted boards have MD > 72, so for these heuristics (all >= MD)
they add at most 4.7e-28. Check: `campaign score --heuristic wd
--rescore-tail` reproduces the recorded WD tail sums to every printed digit.

6. Reading
----------
1. eta: cWD 2.711e-21, LM2 2.343e-21, cLM2 1.641e-21 (uniform, ±0.1%). In
   the thesis's reading of eta ratios, cLM2 expands 1.65 times fewer nodes
   than cWD and 1.43 times fewer than LM2 on hard instances; cWD expands 6.9
   times fewer than WD. For LM2 that reading is approximate (not consistent,
   section 2).
2. The tiers agree with cWD exactly for k <= 7. The cLM2/cWD ratio of eta_k
   falls from 0.98 at k = 8 to about 0.83 by k = 16..24 (exact) and is
   mostly 0.5-0.7 over sampled k = 36..64 (range 0.42-0.87): the tiers gain
   most on the deep spheres that dominate eta.
3. Per-sphere eta_k peaks near 1.0-1.2e-22 (cWD) and 6e-23 (cLM2) around
   k = 48..55, and 6-8% of eta lies at d >= 65.
4. For all three tiers the stored sphere samples agree with the level
   estimates within their intervals in every band (|z| <= 0.7), unlike MD,
   where the same samples are 11% low (records/eta24_md.txt section 6e).
