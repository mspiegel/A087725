eta24 probe — reach probabilities and estimator dispersion (2026-09-16)
=====================================================================

Machine: M2 Pro, 12 threads, on AC, not idle (load average 2.6-3.8). Times are
thread-time sums except "wall".

The instrument
--------------
`eta24 probe` (src/bin/eta24.rs) walks k steps from GOAL (LongMoveDfa window 14,
moribund on, prefix checkpoints every 8), accepts boards proven at distance k
(zpdb k6), and for each accepted board v computes the reach probability P(v)
(src/puzzle24/eta/reach.rs): a backward search from v bounded at k marks the
interval of boards on length-k paths, and a forward pass over (board, walker
node) pushes probability from GOAL through it. Per depth it reports:

  stage A           walk + rejection search, per attempt
  stage B           reach probability, per accepted board
  interval          boards on some length-k path between GOAL and v
  P(v)/walk         reach probability over the probability of the walk taken
                    (1 when only one allowed path reaches v)
  |V_k|, eta_k      Horvitz-Thompson: mean over attempts of 1/P(v) and of
                    w(blank) b^-MD(v) / P(v) (zero for rejected attempts),
                    eta_k divided by 25!/2; 95% intervals from the per-attempt
                    sample variance
  ESS, largest      Kish effective sample size of the nonzero terms, and the
                    largest single term's share of the sum

Correctness checks behind it (unit tests, src/puzzle24/eta/reach.rs):
- P(v) equals the summed probability of exhaustively enumerated walks for
  every board at k = 7, 9, 11, moribund on and off (relative error < 1e-12).
- Horvitz-Thompson over 6,000 sampled walks recovers |V_12| = 24,098 and the
  exact sum of b^-MD over V_12 (from BFS layers) within four standard errors.

Reading
-------
1. The estimators are consistent where references are exact or tight: |V_k|
   within 1 SE of A090031 at k = 12, 20, 30 and of the thesis at 45, 56.
2. The 1/P(v) weights are heavy-tailed. The effective sample size of |V_k| is
   6% of accepted boards at k = 30, 0.7% at 45, 0.6% at 56; single boards
   carry 10-32% of the sum from k = 45 up. P(v) varies through the number of
   allowed paths to v (median 5, up to ~500 at k = 64) and through the product
   of per-step choice counts along them.
3. eta_k adds a second heavy tail: within a sphere, the few boards with low
   Manhattan distance dominate b^-MD. ESS of eta_k is at most 98 of 4,000 even
   at k = 12, where every P(v)/walk ratio is 1.
4. With ESS in single digits the normal intervals and the projected attempt
   counts understate the error. A heavy right tail makes typical sample means
   fall below the true mean. The k = 64 estimates (|V_64| 2.2 SE below the
   thesis; eta_64 an order of magnitude below the ~2.5e-21 per-sphere average
   implied by the exact total of ~1e-19 spread over spheres 30-64) look like
   that.
5. Stage B is ~20% of thread time per accepted board at k = 64 (355 ms against
   ~1.4 s of stage A per accepted board). Its cost is the backward search
   (1.7M nodes mean, 32M max) proving which boards lie on length-k paths; the
   interval itself is small (560 boards mean).
