  k8        the solver's k8 tier: max(Σ normal, Σ reflected) over the three
            8-tile zero-aware PDBs data/pdb24_k8_{a,b,c}.zbin (tiles
            1-4,6-9 / 5,10,14,15,19,20,23,24 / 11-13,16-18,21,22), 30.5 GB

2. Evaluation and checks
------------------------
engine::K8Ctx::eval computes the value engine::seed_k8 seeds (cold lookups,
no arena or cache). Ignored test k8_eval_matches_seed_and_is_admissible:
equals seed_k8 and is >= Manhattan distance on all 18,174 boards within 11
moves and 2,000 uniform boards, and <= true distance on the former.
clm2k8 = max(TierEval cLM2, K8Ctx::eval).

Consistency (scratch, 200,000 random-walk boards, 670,605 neighbour pairs):
|h(s) - h(s')| > 1 never for k8 or clm2k8; h parity always the blank-cell
parity.

3. Method and cost (data/eta24_md/{k8_run,cascade_run}.log)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Pilot (data/eta24_md/pilot_total_k8.*): k8 costs ~5 thread-ms per uniform
random board (page faults: 30.5 GB of tables in 32 GB of RAM), 0.03-0.3 ms
per stored sphere board (k = 25..64), 23 µs per board at k = 20. With 2,000
draws per Manhattan level the level estimate was ±18%: within a level, eta is
carried by rare low-k8 boards.

the same settings reproduce the recorded tail to 0.01%.

6. Reading
----------
1. eta: k8 2.919e-23, max(cLM2, k8) 2.589e-23 (uniform, ±2.0%). In the
   thesis's reading of eta ratios, k8 expands 56 times fewer nodes than cLM2
   on hard instances, and adding cLM2 to k8 cuts another 1.13 times.
2. k8's eta_k peaks near 1e-24 around k = 40 and falls to 8e-26 by k = 64;
   1.6% of eta lies at d >= 65. The WD tiers peak later (k = 48..55) and
   keep 6-8% beyond 64 (records/eta24_tiers.txt).
3. cLM2's contribution inside the cascade is largest near the goal:
   clm2k8/k8 per sphere is 0.80-0.86 for k = 15..32 and mostly 0.92-0.97
   for k = 49..64.
4. For k8 the sphere samples are precise (±2%) and the Manhattan-level
   estimate is the noisy check (±16% per band); both methods agree in every
   band (|z| <= 0.6).
