Manhattan-distance heuristic quality eta on the 24-puzzle — replication (2026-09-16)
===================================================================================

Target: Clausecker, "The Quality of Heuristic Functions for IDA*", ZIB Report
20-17 (papers/ZR-20-17.pdf), and Clausecker & Schintke, SoCS 2021
(papers/00002-SoCS.ClauseckerR.pdf), which report for the Manhattan heuristic:

  eta = 9.926e-20 ± 9.013e-21 (9.08%, 95%)          Table 5.1 / SoCS Table 1
  eta_>=65 = 2.364e-20                              Fig. 5.3 caption
  per-sphere eta_k, k = 0..64                       Fig. 5.3 (chart only)
and, heuristic-independent,
  eta_perfect = 2.5063e-24 and its per-sphere chart Eq. 5.1, Fig. 5.2

Definitions used here: eta = |V|^-1 sum_v w(v) b^-h(v), |V| = 25!/2, b the
asymptotic branching factor with inverse-move pruning. The paper does not state
b or w. b = 2.367604543724 (eta::weights::branching_factor; refitting the
paper's eta_perfect from sphere sizes gives 2.36760). Three blank-cell
weightings are reported throughout: uniform, search-tree equilibrium, degree.

1. The total, exactly
---------------------
Manhattan distance is a sum of per-tile terms, so sum_v w(v) b^-MD(v) over all
25! placements is the permanent of the 25x25 matrix M[t][c] = b^-md(t, c) with
the blank row holding w(c). Solvable placements are those with
sgn(sigma) (-1)^taxicab(blank) = +1, so the sum over V is (perm(M) + det(M'))/2
with M' the blank row signed by taxicab parity. perm by subset DP (no
cancellation), det by LU and by signed subset DP (agree). Checked against
brute force over all 181,440 states of the 8-puzzle (10 digits, three
weightings, two values of b). Computed outside the repo (scratch C program,
2 s); the values are the EXACT_MD_ETA constants in src/bin/eta24.rs.

  weighting   exact eta (b = 2.367604543724)   exact eta (b = 2.36761)   vs published 9.926e-20
  uniform     1.0009629701e-19                 1.0008969759e-19          +0.84%
  tree        9.7305423082e-20                 9.7299009826e-20          -1.97%
  degree      9.7565321490e-20                 9.7558889795e-20          -1.71%

All three lie inside the published 95% interval [9.025e-20, 1.0827e-19].
The solvability correction det(M')/2 is ~1e-9 of the total.

2. eta_perfect and Fig. 5.2, exactly
------------------------------------
eta_perfect = sum_k |V_k|/|V| b^-k with A090031 (k <= 30) and the thesis's
App. B sizes (31..64): 2.50627e-24 at b = 2.367604543724 (published 2.5063e-24).
Fig. 5.2 read from the PDF's vector drawing (next section) agrees point by point
with |V_k|/|V| b^-k to within the drawing's coordinate resolution: largest
difference 4.7e-29 against a peak of 1.3e-25, sum 2.50699e-24.

3. Fig. 5.3, digitized
----------------------
The chart is a vector polyline in the PDF (content stream at byte offset 91161).
Mapping by its gridlines and ticks gives records/eta24_fig53_digitized.tsv
(quantum ~1.1e-24; one quantum of baseline offset removed). The same procedure
reproduces Fig. 5.2 exactly (section 2), so the digitization adds no bias
beyond the quantum.

The paper is internally inconsistent by about 9%: the Fig. 5.3 points sum to
8.42e-20 over k = 0..64; with the stated eta_>=65 = 2.364e-20 that is 1.079e-19,
whereas Table 5.1 gives 9.926e-20 (which implies sum_0..64 = 7.562e-20).

4. Per-sphere eta_k by stratified sampling
------------------------------------------
Method (src/puzzle24/eta, src/bin/eta24.rs `campaign`):
- k = 0..24: exact breadth-first layers (`campaign layers --max-k 24`, 14 s,
  14 GiB peak); |V_k| equals A090031 at every k.
- k = 25..64: random walks from GOAL under LongMoveDfa window 14 (15-move rule),
  moribund pruning, lookahead move choice (records/eta24_probe.txt, Result 4);
  a walk is accepted when a bounded IDA* with the 6-6-6-6 zero-aware PDB proves
  no shorter solution (prefix checks every 8 steps); its reach probability P(v)
  comes from the interval DP (eta::reach). Horvitz-Thompson means over all
  attempts; 95% intervals from 20 round-robin batches of chunks.
- Tail eta_>=65 = exact total (section 1) - sum over k = 0..64.

Validation of the whole pipeline against exact values: a small campaign at
k = 15..20 (scratch) matched exact layers in 23 of 24 size and eta_k
comparisons at 95%; sizes k = 25..30 match A090031 within 1.3 SE.

Reading
-------
1. The published total (Table 5.1) is reproduced exactly: 1.0010e-19 against
   9.926e-20 ± 9.08%.
2. Fig. 5.2 and eta_perfect are reproduced exactly from sphere sizes.
3. The published tail eta_>=65 = 2.364e-20 is reproduced within error:
   2.54e-20 ± 26% (uniform).
4. The per-sphere histogram reproduces Fig. 5.3 in shape. Its level is 0.887 of
   the figure's. The paper's own figure sums to 1.114 times its table
   (8.42e-20 against 7.56e-20 implied by Table 5.1), and our sum agrees with the
   table to 1.2%, so the offset against the figure is the figure's, as far as
   this measurement can tell.
5. The weighting cannot be identified from the published numbers: uniform,
   tree and degree totals differ by 2.8%, inside every published and measured
   interval. Fig. 5.2 (eta_perfect) matches the unweighted sphere sizes.

6. The tail measured directly, and the sphere sum (2026-09-17)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Section 6 measures eta_>=65 instead of taking it by subtraction. It revises
readings 3 and 4 of section 5 (see "Reading, revised" below).

(a) Uniform tail. `campaign tail --min-distance 65`: 710,934,528 uniform
solvable draws, 15,027 thread-s. Sampled tail 1.6514e-20 ± 44.1%; sum
(section 5) + tail = 9.1219e-20, -8.87% (-1.8 SE) against exact. The estimate
rests on a handful of boards: one accepted board with MD 31 is 21% of it, and
the 1,258 with MD <= 40 are 80%. All 46 with MD <= 36 were re-proven >= 65 by
the ZPDB search, and the three lowest (MD 31, 33, 33) by solve24's cWD engine
(`--prove-at-least 65`).

(b) Where eta_MD lies. eta::md_levels counts the placements N_m with Manhattan
distance m exactly (subset DP; 10.2 GiB at m <= 40, 2.5 s), and
eta_MD = Σ_m N_m b^-m / 25!. Levels m <= 40 hold 9.65e-20 (96%) on 2.7e-6 of
states; m <= 30 hold 6.23e-20 (62%) on 1.8e-9 of states, which a uniform draw
reaches about once per 5e8.

ones count as zero) and weighted by N_m/|V|. Checks against exact values:

Reading, revised
----------------
1. The published total is reproduced exactly (unchanged).
2. Our sphere sum over 0..64 is 11.3% low, outside its batch-means interval
   (-2.9 SE), and the shortfall grows with depth: -8% at 25..40, -12% at
   41..64. The reach weights are heavy-tailed at depth (eta24_probe.txt,
   Results 2-3), so batch means under-cover. This replaces reading 4 of
   section 5: the figure-versus-table offset is not the figure's.
3. Fig. 5.3 agrees with exact - tail to 0.03% over 0..64.
4. The published eta_>=65 = 2.364e-20 is 1.49 times the level estimate
   1.584e-20 ± 2.1%. With the figure's 8.42e-20 it would give a total 7.8%
   above exact; Table 5.1's total is within 1% of exact. Section 5's tail by
   subtraction (2.54e-20 ± 26%) inherited the sphere-sum shortfall; that is
   why it matched the published tail (replaces reading 3).
5. For eta_MD, uniform tail draws are the weak part of the thesis's method:
   their estimate is set by the few MD ~ 30 boards drawn. Stratifying by
   Manhattan level measures the tail to ±2.1% in 7 thread-hours.
