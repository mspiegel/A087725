eta24 — sphere-walk yield and rejection cost (2026-09-16)
========================================================

Machine: M2 Pro, 12 threads (rayon default), on AC, NOT idle (Chrome, Defender,
load average ~6 before runs). Times are thread-time sums, so contention inflates
them; they are for choosing between levers that differ by several-fold, not for
percent-level A/Bs.

The instrument
--------------
`eta24 yield` (src/bin/eta24.rs) walks k steps from GOAL under the walker rule
(src/puzzle24/eta/walker.rs: legal moves minus reversal minus the MoveDfa W=11
prune mask, optionally with moribund pruning) and accepts the end board iff no
solution of length <= k-2 exists (src/puzzle24/eta/sphere.rs, reject_shorter).

  yield              accepted / attempts
  nodes, us/attempt  verification search cost, including rejected walks
  ms/accepted        thread time per accepted sample = us/attempt / yield

Clausecker's yields (ZIB Report 20-17, App. B) used an FSM matching segments up
to n = 22 (n = 24 at k = 63, 64) with moribund tracking.

Dead-end rate with moribund off rises from 0.7% (k=10) to 28.5% (k=64); with
moribund on it is 0 at every depth. The walker table has 39,033 nodes, 81 of
them doomed. Moribund pruning raises yield by 10-50% relative, less than the
thesis's "up to 58%" over plain FSM pruning, and at k = 64 the two are within
noise. The W = 11 DFA yields 0.4-0.5x the thesis's n = 22-24 FSM from k = 40 on.

Result 2 — verifier choice, k = 64, moribund on, 2,000 attempts
---------------------------------------------------------------
                           nodes/attempt   ms/attempt
  zpdb k6 (6-6-6-6 + refl)       223,638        33.4
  zpdb k7 (7-7-7-3 + refl)       671,405       110.4
  cwd (engine::bounded)        4,911,080       198.7

Identical seeds give identical yields in all three (the verdicts agree). The
larger k7 partition expands 3x more nodes than k6 on these boards. The engine
over plain cWD expands 22x more and also pays ~0.8 ms per call of fixed setup
(visible at k = 16: 792 us/attempt for 3.9 nodes).

Result 3 — prefix checkpoints, moribund on, zpdb k6
---------------------------------------------------
A walk whose j-step prefix is already closer than j can never be accepted, so
testing prefixes every c steps back from k rejects early without changing any
verdict (test: sphere::tests::checkpoints_do_not_change_outcomes).

Spacing 6-8 is the flat optimum: 5.4x cheaper per attempt at k = 64 and 2.1x at

time per accepted sample, down from 8.6 s.
